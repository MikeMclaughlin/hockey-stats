How the edge is scored

  • Season record — regulation/OT wins–losses–OT losses. Compared on points percentage.
  • PDO — shooting % + save % from shots on goal. ~100 is league-normal; much higher is finishing/save luck.
  • Avg GD last 5 — (GF − GA) / games in each club’s last five completed regular-season games.
  • SOG share — shots on goal for / (SF + SA).
  • Expected vs actual — MoneyPuck xGoals vs actual goals (GF − xGF). Positive means outscoring the shot quality.
  • CF% — Corsi for percentage: shot attempts for / (CF + CA), all situations.
  • HDCF% — high-danger chances for / (HDCF + HDCA).
  • 5v5 xGF% — MoneyPuck 5-on-5 expected goals for / (xGF + xGA).
  • Estimated score — projected goals from season scoring rate, xG rate, last-5 form, HDCF tilt, and a small home-ice bump.
  • Moneyline / O/U — NHL partner board for the US (DraftKings when posted). A “≠ DK” mark means the model edge and the DraftKings favorite are different teams.
  • Seasons — the scoreboard season is the slate on the board. Records and rates use the latest season that already has a final regular-season game, for every date, and that choice stays. Before any final, they stay on the previous regular season. Early in the season the sample is small. A team with no games in the stats season shows 0–0–0 and blank rates.
  • Updates — a scheduled job refreshes the board, re-locks estimates before puck drop, and grades finals. Opening the page is not required.
